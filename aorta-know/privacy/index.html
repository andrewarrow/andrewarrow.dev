Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

This policy covers the Aorta Know website. It explains what happens when you visit these pages or contact support.

Visiting this website

This is a static information website. It has no accounts, forms, advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. Its code does not set cookies, use browser storage, or ask for access to your health records, location, camera, or microphone.

Images, fonts, styles, and scripts are served locally or supplied by your device. Browsing the site does not load third-party services. The small script on the home page only controls visual transitions.

Technical requests and hosting

When a browser requests a page or an asset, it sends standard connection information to the server, including its IP address, the requested address, and browser information. Server logging and the retention of any logs depend on the hosting configuration; they are not controlled by this website’s files.

Contacting support

The support links open your email application. No message is sent until you choose to send it. If you email support@andrewarrow.dev, the recipient receives your email address and the message or attachments you include. Email is also processed by the services used to send and receive it.

Include only what is needed for your question. You do not need to send health readings or exported health files to browse this website. Privacy questions or requests about support correspondence can be sent to the same address.

The Aorta Know app

The product description states that Aorta Know uses heart readings from the iPhone’s Health data store, ideally populated by an Apple Watch. It also describes personal notes, measurement entries, workouts, and health and workout exports.

This website does not receive those records or connect to the app. The available product information does not establish the app’s storage, synchronization, transmission, retention, or deletion practices. This website policy does not make claims about those practices. Contact support for app-specific privacy information.

Changes and contact

If the website’s implementation changes, this policy should be updated to describe it, with a revised date. For questions about this website or its privacy policy, contact support@andrewarrow.dev.

Wristful Thinking / Privacy

Privacy, kept simple.

Your sleep is personal. Wristful Thinking keeps it that way: no advertising, no analytics trackers, no third-party SDKs, and no uploading your health data to our servers.

Last updated October 12, 2026

Your sleep.
Your data.

Sleep and health information

Wristful Thinking uses sleep and related health measurements available through Apple Health to help you understand your nights. Most features require a compatible Apple Watch and Apple Health permissions. Available measurements vary by device and region.

We do not upload your health data to our servers.

No advertising or tracking

Wristful Thinking has no advertising, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs. One payment unlocks the complete app, without subscriptions or additional in-app purchases.

Your choices

You control the Apple Health permissions you grant to Wristful Thinking through your device settings. Features that depend on a measurement need permission to access it.

If you choose to export your data or connect it to a routine, consider where you share that information.

Contact

For privacy questions or app support, email support@andrewarrow.dev. Only include the information needed to describe your question.

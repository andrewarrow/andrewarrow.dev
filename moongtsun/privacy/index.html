Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

This policy describes the moongtsun informational website. It covers what this website does when you visit and what happens when you choose to contact support. It does not describe the app’s complete health-data processing practices.

Information on this website

The website consists of static pages and locally hosted images and styles. It has no account sign-in, forms, payments, analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or third-party embeds. It does not request or read Apple Watch or Apple Health data.

The website’s code does not set cookies or use local storage, session storage or a browser database. Images, the logo and styles are served with the pages; there are no external font or image requests. Your browser may cache these static files according to its normal settings.

Website requests and hosting

To deliver a page, your browser sends a request to the website’s hosting service. That request includes technical information such as your IP address, the requested address and browser request headers. The hosting service may keep operational or security logs.

Logging settings, access and retention depend on the hosting service and its configuration, which are not established by this static website’s files. Contact support if you need details about hosting logs or have a request concerning them.

When you email support

Support links open your email application. No message is sent by simply visiting this site or clicking a support link. If you choose to send an email, the recipient receives your email address, your message and any attachments you include, through the email services involved.

Send only what is needed to explain your question. Avoid including health records or other sensitive information unless you choose to share them for your request. For privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence, email support@andrewarrow.dev.

The moongtsun app

The product information supplied for this website describes activity, recovery and readiness insights from Apple Watch health data; sleep data from Apple Sleep or AutoSleep; wellness surveys and workout questionnaires; exports; and optional group sharing. Group sharing lets you choose which data to share and requires a subscription that includes sharing, plus available iCloud storage. You can monitor your own information without group sharing.

The supplied information does not establish the app’s full data flows, storage locations, analytics, security measures, retention periods or deletion procedures. This website makes no additional claims about those practices. Ask support@andrewarrow.dev for the applicable app privacy information before deciding what health data to provide or share.

App subscriptions are purchased through your Apple ID and managed in App Store subscription settings. This website does not collect subscription payments or payment details.

Your choices

You can read this website without submitting personal information through its pages. You control whether to send a support email, what to include, and whether to clear cached website files in your browser. App permissions, sharing and app-data requests are separate from this website; contact support for the applicable controls and policies.

Policy updates and contact

If this website’s implementation changes, this page should be updated to reflect it. The date above identifies the current version.

Website and app support, privacy questions and requests: support@andrewarrow.dev.

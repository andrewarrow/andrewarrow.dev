dayvergent / Privacy

Privacy, kept simple.

dayvergent is made to help you pay attention to the day, not to collect more information about you. This policy explains what the app handles and what stays yours.

01 / Information we handle

dayvergent does not require an account. The app's core features use the date on your device to calculate and display the 13 Moon calendar, Dreamspell Kin, widgets, and related daily information.

If you contact the publisher for support, we receive the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and a description of the issue. We use it only to respond and improve the app.

02 / On-device storage

Your selected dates, preferences, and widget settings may be stored locally on your device so dayvergent can remember them. The calendar calculations themselves can be performed on-device. This information is not sold or shared for advertising.

Removing the app removes its local storage from that device, subject to the operating system's normal backup behavior.

03 / Widgets and system services

When you add a dayvergent Home Screen widget, the operating system makes the widget's displayed information available to that widget. The widget may refresh as the date changes and opens the app when you tap a day.

dayvergent does not need your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera for its calendar features.

04 / Analytics and advertising

dayvergent does not use your calendar activity to serve targeted advertising. If a future version introduces optional analytics or another third-party service, this policy will be updated before that change applies and the relevant service will be identified.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time and remove its local data by deleting it from your device. You can also manage app permissions and system backups through your device settings.

Questions about this policy, privacy requests, or app support can be sent to support@andrewarrow.dev. Please do not include sensitive information in a support message unless it is necessary to describe the issue.

Changes to this policy

When the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new “Last updated” date. The version published here is the current version for the dayvergent website.

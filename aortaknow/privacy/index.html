This page describes the data practices of the AortaKnow website as it is implemented today.

Last updated October 13, 2026

A quiet site.
A clear policy.

What this website does

AortaKnow is presented here as a static informational website. It does not create accounts, accept form submissions, or provide a way to enter health information.

Information we collect

The site contains no advertising, analytics trackers, cookies, or third-party data collection scripts. Visiting these pages does not send health information to AortaKnow.

The site uses locally stored images and stylesheets. It does not request access to your device, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or health data.

Support email

If you email support@andrewarrow.dev, the publisher receives the email address and the contents you choose to include. That information is used to read and respond to your message. Avoid sending sensitive health information unless it is necessary to explain your question.

Changes to this policy

If the website’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date and a description of the change.

AortaKnow is an informational product concept. This website is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for speaking with a qualified clinician.

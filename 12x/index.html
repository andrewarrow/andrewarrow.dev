A few details

Good to know.

Which devices and years does 12x support?+

12x runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later. You can plan each year from 2027 through 2032, with a separate saved plan for every year.

Do I need to know the exact dates?+

Just the month. 12x helps you make room for experiences across the year. Big experiences span multiple days; the other sizes are day outings. You can figure out dates and bookings separately.

Can I use it without location access?+

Yes. Choose your state manually at any time. Optional approximate location access simply helps 12x select your state when you browse destinations.

Where are my plans saved?+

On your device, with no account required. The planner and bundled destination list work offline; destination websites and automatic state detection may need an internet connection. Read the privacy policy for details.

Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This page explains what happens when you visit the Group Chat 3000 website or contact us for support.

Visiting this website

This is a static marketing website. It has no sign-in, message forms, advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. The website's code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Images and styles are served with the website, and fonts come from your device.

The website does not connect to your Group Chat 3000 account or access your conversations, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Hosting and technical information

When your browser requests a page or image, the hosting provider receives standard connection information such as your IP address, the requested address, and browser information. The provider may process this information to deliver the website, maintain security, and investigate technical problems. Server logging and retention depend on the hosting provider and configuration.

Contacting us

If you email help@andrewarrow.dev, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support. Email providers also process the correspondence to send and receive it.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to address the request, maintain relevant records, or meet legal obligations. Please include only information needed to explain your question; do not send passwords or private group conversations.

Privacy in the app

This website policy does not describe how in-app accounts, messages, or media are stored or transmitted. Andrew Arrow's general privacy policy provides information about our apps and related services, including information you provide, device permissions, sharing, and deletion requests.

Check the privacy information accompanying your version of the app and its App Store listing. Contact us if you need details about a particular app feature or how its data is handled.

Your choices and requests

You can ask about personal information we hold or request access, correction, or deletion by emailing help@andrewarrow.dev. Include “Group Chat 3000” and enough information to identify your request. We may need to verify your identity. We cannot retrieve information that remains solely on your device, and legal or security obligations may limit what can be deleted.

External links

Links to the App Store, the developer's website, and email services take you to services governed by their own privacy policies. Clicking a support email link opens your email application; it does not send a message until you choose to send it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its revision date when the website's practices change. For questions about privacy or this policy, contact Andrew Arrow at help@andrewarrow.dev.
